# Screen saver art

- `dvd-logo.png`: made by `tools/make_dvd_logo.py` from Wikimedia Commons
  [File:DVD-Video Logo.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:DVD-Video_Logo.svg),
  public domain (simple text and shapes). The DVD Video logo is a trademark of DVD
  Format/Logo Licensing Corporation. Batoplex is not affiliated with that company and uses
  the logo as a tribute to the idle screens of DVD players.
- `saver/*.png` (Flying Objects, Aquarium, Night City): new pixel art for Batoplex, released
  under the repository's MIT license. The first drafts came from an image model. Each
  sheet was then reduced to a pixel grid and a small palette and checked by hand. No sprite,
  frame or palette is taken from another screen saver.
